# A Book of Voices — the keepsake book plan

A 90th-birthday book from 20+ children, grandchildren and great-grandchildren,
built so every message exists **twice**: written on the page, and spoken aloud
behind a QR code. The written word is the keepsake; the voice is the part that
makes her cry.

---

## The anatomy of a page

Each contributor gets one page (great-grandkids can share a spread). A page holds:

- **A photo** of the contributor — ideally with her, if one exists.
- **Their written note**, answering one chosen prompt (below).
- **A QR code** in the corner. Scanning it opens that person's short clip —
  their actual voice or a little video — hosted by the companion app.
- **Their name and relationship** ("Sarah, eldest granddaughter").

A small printed line near the first QR — "Scan with your phone camera to hear
us" — saves confusion for a 90-year-old opening it for the first time.

## How the book is ordered

Open with a **family tree** as the first spread: her at the root, then the
branches. Then flow the pages by generation — her children first, then
grandchildren, then great-grandchildren. Turning the pages literally walks
through the family she made. Close with a single full-width photo of everyone,
or her favourite photo of herself young.

## The prompts (this is the important part)

With 20+ people, a blank "write something nice" invites 20 versions of "Happy
birthday Grandma, we love you." Assign or let each person pick **one** prompt so
the book reads like a chorus of different angles:

- A memory only I have of you
- Something you taught me without meaning to
- A saying of yours I still use
- The first thing I picture when I think of you
- What I want my own children to know about you
- A time you made me laugh

Great-grandchildren: have them answer "What makes Grandma special?" in their own
words, or draw a picture. Unedited kid-logic is always the showstopper — keep
their spelling exactly as-is.

## Production

1. Collect notes, photos and clips (the app handles this — see the README).
2. Build pages in any photobook tool — Mixbook, Artifact Uprising, Milk Books,
   Snapfish, Shutterfly. Drop in photo + note + the downloaded QR PNG per page.
3. Order one beautiful hardcover. Consider a second copy — once people see it,
   someone always wants one.

>

> • Something she taught you without meaning to
> • A saying of hers you still use
> • The first thing you picture when you think of her
> • What you want your own kids to know about her
> • A time she made you laugh

>

>
> 🗓️ Please by **[deadline]** so we can print in time.
>
> Don't overthink it — short and from the heart beats long and polished. Thank

### Organizer tips

- **One deadline, and chase people.** Two-thirds will submit after a reminder.
  Plan to nudge twice.
- **Collect early, print late.** Photobook tools take 1–2 weeks to ship; leave
  buffer.
- **Test one QR end-to-end** (print it, scan with an actual phone) before
  ordering the whole book.
- **Back everything up.** Once collected, download all clips and photos to a
  folder you keep — the app is the delivery mechanism, not your only copy.
- **Keep the app live.** The QR codes point to your deployed site; don't change
  the domain or take it down after printing, or the codes stop working.
